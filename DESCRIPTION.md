# Vantage — submission text

## Design description (for the Contra form)

Vantage is a Video.js v10 skin for watching play, not films. Gameplay has angles, rounds and moments you want to jump between, so Vantage is a multiview player: the main POV takes the stage and two more stay live beside it in a 70/30, 50/50 or stage-only layout. Click one or press 1–3 and it swaps onto the stage at the same timestamp, every angle on one clock, and W opens the whole multiview fullscreen with nothing else on screen. The seek bar is a lane of event pins: a skull for an elimination, a crown for a clutch, a flag for an objective, a tag for each round, in the colour of the side that scored, solid once played and outlined ahead. Tab to them, or press N for the next highlight and R for an instant replay of the last eight seconds at half speed. A kill feed calls the action, a broadcast HUD carries the score and angle, spoiler-free mode hides every result for late viewers, and any moment can be shared as a link. It works by keyboard, touch and mouse, collapses to a chip row on phones where only the stage streams, keeps its glyphs in forced colours and goes still under reduced motion.

## Three first lines for the social post

1. Video players are built for films. I built one for matches: a Video.js v10 skin with live angles you swap in one click.
2. Press 1, 2 or 3 to change the camera. N jumps to the next kill, R replays it at half speed. Vantage is a Video.js v10 skin for watching play.
3. What if the seek bar knew where the round started? Vantage turns the Video.js v10 timeline into a map of the match.
