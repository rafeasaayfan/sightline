# Vantage — submission text

## Design description (for the Contra form)

Vantage is a Video.js v10 skin for watching play, not films. Gameplay has angles, rounds and moments you want to jump between, so Vantage is a multiview player: the main angle takes the stage and two more stay live beside it in a 70/30, 50/50 or stage-only layout — click one, or press 1–3, and it swaps onto the stage at the same timestamp, with every angle on one clock. The seek bar becomes an event timeline: eliminations, objectives and round starts are markers, told apart by shape as well as colour, that you can tab to and jump between, and N skips to the next highlight. A broadcast-style HUD carries the scoreboard and the active angle on solid chips without covering the action, the page writes a match summary with a momentum chart from the same data, and every control sits on a scrim measured to stay readable over a white frame and a black one. It works by keyboard, touch and mouse, collapses to a chip row on phones where only the stage streams, and goes still under reduced motion.

## Three first lines for the social post

1. Video players are built for films. I built one for matches: a Video.js v10 skin with live angles you swap in one click.
2. Press 1, 2 or 3 to change the camera. Press N to jump to the next elimination. Vantage is a Video.js v10 skin for watching play.
3. What if the seek bar knew where the round started? Vantage turns the Video.js v10 timeline into a map of the match.
