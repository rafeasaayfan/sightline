# Sightline — submission text

## Design description (for the Contra form)

Sightline is a Video.js v10 skin for watching play, not films. Gameplay has angles, rounds and moments you want to jump between, so Sightline is a multiview player: the main POV fills the stage, two more play live beside it at exactly its height, and the controls sit in one panel under all three. Click an angle or press 1–3 and it swaps onto the stage at the same timestamp; W opens the whole multiview fullscreen. The seek bar is a lane of event pins (a skull for a kill, a crown for a clutch, a flag for an objective, a tag for each round) in the colour of the side that scored, with a frame preview on hover. N jumps to the next highlight, R replays the last eight seconds at half speed, a kill feed calls the action, and you can share a moment or cut a clip with a start and an end, or switch on spoiler-free for a late watch. It works by keyboard, touch and mouse, keeps its glyphs in forced colours, and goes still under reduced motion.

## Three first lines for the social post

1. Video players are built for films. I built one for matches: a Video.js v10 skin with live angles you swap in one click.
2. Press 1, 2 or 3 to change the camera. N jumps to the next kill, R replays it at half speed. Sightline is a Video.js v10 skin for watching play.
3. What if the seek bar knew where the round started? Sightline turns the Video.js v10 timeline into a map of the match.
