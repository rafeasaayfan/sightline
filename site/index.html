Match recap

every event is a seek — click to jump

    Players don't watch a film. They watch a match.

    Every video player is designed for a story that goes one way. Gameplay has angles, rounds and moments you want to jump between. Vantage is a Video.js v10 skin built for that.

    One stage, two synced angles you can swap with a click or the keys 1–3, and a timeline where every elimination, objective and round is a marker you can seek to. Everything works by keyboard, touch and mouse.

    Details

    • Angles are real controls

      Tiles and chips swap the source on the stage at the same timestamp. The other angles stay muted and keep in sync within a quarter second.

    • A timeline of moments

      Eliminations, objectives and rounds come from a WebVTT file. Markers differ by shape and colour, carry tooltips, and are one Tab stop away; N jumps to the next.

    • Keyboard like a spectator client

      1–3 angles, N next highlight, Space, M, F, arrows, and ? for the full list. Focus is always visible and the band never hides while something in it has focus.

    • Readable over anything

      Controls sit on a gradient scrim and the HUD on solid chips, never on raw footage. Measured over white and black frames: 4.5:1 or better for every label.

    • Phone first

      On a phone the angles become a chip row under the stage, tap targets grow to 48 px, and only the stage streams, which saves data and battery.

    • Calm on request

      Under reduced motion toasts and the sync pulse go still and swaps cut instead of fade. Under forced colours the markers keep their shapes.
