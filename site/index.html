Spectator mode a Video.js v10 skin for esports broadcasts, scrims and game VODs

Watch the match,not the movie.

Sightline turns a Video.js player into a spectator client. One POV fills the stage, two more views play beside it and stay live, a kill feed calls the action, and the seek bar is a lane of events you can jump to.

    01

    Built for spectators

    Everything a viewer needs to follow a match, and nothing a film player needs instead.

    • Angles on the stage

      Two more views play beside the stage, live and in sync: here an aim cam and the radar, or other players. Click one or press 1–3 and it swaps in at the same moment.

    • An event lane

      Every kill, clutch, objective and round is a pin above the seek bar, in the colour of the side that scored, and hovering shows the frame. N jumps to the next.

    • Instant replay

      R plays the last eight seconds at half speed, then hands back to the match where you left it.

    • Multiview fullscreen

      W fills the screen with the stage and its angles, and nothing else: no page, no navigation.

    • Kill feed and HUD

      A game-style feed calls each event as it happens; the scoreboard and active angle sit on solid chips.

    • Spoiler-free and shareable

      Hide every score and event for a late watch, or copy a link that opens on this exact moment and angle.

    03

    Accessible on purpose

    Measured, not assumed. The full audit, item by item, is in the repository.

    • 100Lighthouse accessibility
    • 5.28 : 1lowest text contrast, over a pure white frame
    • 48 pxtouch targets on phones
    • < 0.01layout shift while the page loads
    • Keyboard first. Every control in visual order with one visible ring; menus close with Esc and hand focus back.
    • Never colour alone. Every event has its own glyph; "on" states change shape or label too.
    • Calm on request. Reduced motion stops the sweeps, the pulse and the spinner; swaps cut instead of fade.
    • Forced colours. System colours throughout; event pins keep their glyphs; focus uses Highlight.

    04

    Install

    Sightline is skin source for Video.js 10: copy it into your project and keep the public properties.

    1. Add Video.js 10 and the fonts

      npm i @videojs/html @videojs/mux-video @fontsource/inter @fontsource/barlow-condensed @fontsource/jetbrains-mono

    2. Copy the skin and import it

      import '@videojs/html/video/player';
import '@videojs/html/media/mux-video';
import './sightline/video/skin';   // src/ from the repository

    3. Add your stream and its events

      <video-player>
  <!-- skin.html markup, with your media where it says -->
  <mux-video src="https://stream.mux.com/PLAYBACK_ID.m3u8" crossorigin>
    <track kind="metadata" label="events" src="events.vtt" default>
  </mux-video>
</video-player>
